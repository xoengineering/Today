# Privacy Policy

Today is a view on your Reminders. It stores nothing of its own, anywhere.

- The app reads and writes reminders in two lists in the Reminders app, Today and Backlog, on your device. Those reminders sync between your devices through your own iCloud account, under Apple's terms, the same as they would without Today.
- The app has no server. It makes no network connections of its own.
- The app collects no analytics, no crash reports, no identifiers, and no usage data. Nothing about you or your reminders leaves your device because of Today.
- The app's settings, such as the end-of-day time, are stored on the device that set them.

Today is open source. The code is at [github.com/xoengineering/Today](https://github.com/xoengineering/Today), and this policy describes that code.

Questions go to the repository's issues page.
